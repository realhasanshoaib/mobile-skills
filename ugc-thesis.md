# UGC thesis

Source: [Mau Baron’s X post](https://fxtwitter.com/maubaron/status/2085742493077741599)

User-generated content is a growth system, not a one-off viral trick. Make the app worth sharing, hire creators with proven reach, measure which hooks convert, and turn the winners into paid ads. Reinvest the returns into more creators and more distribution.

The loop is:

**UGC views → downloads → revenue → ad budget → more growth.**

The practical order:

1. Build a product with a screenshot-worthy feature, a clear one-line pitch, and strong onboarding and retention.
2. Start with a few creators who already know the niche and can make good content. Pay for output and performance, not follower count.
3. Track views, engagement, comments, payouts, downloads, and conversion. Find the winning hooks and replicate them across the creator team.
4. Turn the best organic content into broad paid campaigns. Start small, measure return on ad spend, and scale gradually.

The post claims this approach grew revenue from $25,000 to $130,000 per month, alongside 50–80 million views and 450,000 downloads. Those figures are the author’s reported results, not independently verified benchmarks.
